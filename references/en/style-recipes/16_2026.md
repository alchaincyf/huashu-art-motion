## Art style (Dribbble flat illustration + grain)
- Solid color blocks, no outlines; volume is separated only by slightly darker blocks of the same hue (diagonal sweater highlight `#f08b7d`, cat thigh `#f3ad4c` + one dark curved edge).
- **Grain stippling `stipple()`**: scatter 2px square dots within shapes, density a position function: pink circle `0.12 + 0.75·clamp((y-cy+120)/420)` (denser lower down); pale-purple block and sun denser toward lower-right. Cache all static parts in `fl_bg`.
- After drawing the character layer, overlay static grain using `source-atop` (5% dark dots α70, 3.5% light dots α90), so grain falls only on characters.
- Light cone: 26000 dots, width increasing linearly with y from 56→150, opacity decreasing with y, denser near center; cache as a 340×420 texture, swinging with the pendant light around (1010,0).

## Girl (custom poses on RIG)
- Torso/neck anchors: `RIG.girl({s:1.12})`; adjust head separately using `RIG.girl({s:1.344, dy:43.7})`, enlarging it 1.2× about the neck for flat illustration's big-head proportions. Face, bangs, rear hair, bun, eye/lip/ear anchors all come from it.
- Arms: `RIG.ik2(肩, 目标, 150, 140, -1)` (shoulder, target), blending three target sets: typing (hands alternate 7px at 22 rad/s on keyboard) → petting cat (688–704, near hand draws small circles on cat's back) → gasp (791–798, `ease.outBack` raises hand before chin, fingers spread).
- Expression: closed-eye arcs + smiling mouth (<686) → dot eyes + highlights, small o mouth (from 686, o pops with outBack) → big eyes + wide mouth + tongue (from 791) → squeezed "<" eyes after frame 838 (reference final frame also has closed eyes).
- Stress lines: three radiating from above/in front of head, length pops with outBack; color 793 pale pink → 796 full red (RGB interpolation); 830–888 random jitter ±2.5px every two frames, head jitter ±2px in sync.
- Draw trouser legs/socks/shoes separately (RIG wears a long skirt).

## Cat (pose library + interpolation)
Each pose is local keypoints (faces right, origin = centroid): body ten points, white chest six points, thigh ellipse, three back-stripe segments, head, two forelegs, hind leg, tail four Bézier points, gy (feet-to-centroid distance).
Poses: `SIT_F` (floor sitting, measured at reference 10.95s), `SIT_T` (table sitting, measured facing left then mirrored to store facing right), `LEAP` (horizontally elongated), `LAND` (front paws down). `mixPose` lerps each number.
Transform: `translate(质心) → [绕脚底 或 绕质心] rotate → scale(dir·sx, sy)` (centroid; rotate around feet or centroid). Squash/stretch pivots around feet, so crouching does not lift them off the floor.

## Cup and shattering
- Cup x: linear interpolation through 16 measured shot-list points (744:981 … 796:801); begins tilting outward -0.12 over 790–796.
- Fall 796–815: Catmull-Rom through nine measured center points (measured gravity ≈1.75px/frame²), rotation `-0.12 - 2.3·q^1.3`; six coffee drops trail from past cup positions spaced 1.3 frames apart, earlier drops smaller.
- Impact 815: seven radial impact lines fade over eight frames; two large shards use Lagrange parabolas through three shot-list points (818/824/834), stop after landing at 834; nine small fragments follow parabolas, stop at individual ground heights 893–911; coffee stain width 818:140 → 822:220 → 826:235 (ease.out); two gray ripples expand to widths 560/460, fade before 838; semicircular handle + tea-bag tag remain on floor.
- Cup-tap motion lines: three short lines right of cup expand and fade over 12 frames after impact.
